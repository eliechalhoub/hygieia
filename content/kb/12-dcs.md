## What it is

Gas bubbles can form in the body when pressure drops, as it does when moving into a lower-pressure spacesuit.

## What Hygieia can observe

A check after every spacewalk in the surface profiles, and a triage tree for joint pain or breathing trouble after EVA.

## What Hygieia cannot observe

Bubbles. Hygieia relies on symptoms the crew report.
