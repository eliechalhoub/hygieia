## What it is

A small crew far from Earth depends on working well together for the whole mission.

## What Hygieia can observe

Shared crew time as a recovery lever, and cooperative team goals with no leaderboard.

## What Hygieia cannot observe

Team dynamics. Hygieia has no measure of how the crew gets along.
