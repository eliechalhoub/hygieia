## What it is

Spacewalks are physically hard work in a pressurised suit and carry a risk of injury and fatigue.

## What Hygieia can observe

Go/no-go before each spacewalk from projected readiness, a reaction test the evening before, and a post-EVA check reminder.

## What Hygieia cannot observe

Suit fit, hand injuries or workload during the spacewalk itself.
