## What it is

In weightlessness body fluids shift toward the head and the heart and vessels adapt to a lower workload. NASA tracks this as a risk for long missions and for the return to gravity.

## What Hygieia can observe

Resting heart rate, heart-rate variability and blood pressure every day, each compared with your own 30-day baseline. Drift shows on the Heart & vessels region.

## What Hygieia cannot observe

Heart structure, vessel stiffness or rhythm problems. Those need ultrasound or an ECG read by a clinician.
