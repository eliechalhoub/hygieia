## What it is

Weight-bearing bones lose mineral in weightlessness. Exercise is the main countermeasure in use.

## What Hygieia can observe

Exercise adherence over 14 days, as a proxy. Below 80% of the prescription raises the bone and muscle alert.

## What Hygieia cannot observe

Bone density. Hygieia does not measure bone at all; the alert is inferred from exercise adherence and says so.
