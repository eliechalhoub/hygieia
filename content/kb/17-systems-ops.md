## What it is

With long communication delays the crew must operate complex systems, including medical ones, with less help from the ground.

## What Hygieia can observe

This is the problem Hygieia is built around: offline triage, standing orders written in advance, and a delayed-link queue.

## What Hygieia cannot observe

Whether the crew has the right training or procedures for systems other than this one.
