## What it is

Beyond low Earth orbit the crew receives a steady dose from galactic cosmic rays, plus bursts from solar particle events.

## What Hygieia can observe

Dose tracking only: a personal dosimeter with a neutron channel, the mission-to-date total against the plan, and real solar particle events replayed from NASA DONKI with a storm-shelter checklist.

## What Hygieia cannot observe

Cancer risk. Hygieia does not estimate risk from dose, and it has no biological measurement of radiation damage.

## Notes

Daily dose rates per environment come from published measurements (MSL RAD, Chang'E 4 LND, ESA DOSIS).
