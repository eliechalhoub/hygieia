## What it is

Spaceflight can change how the immune system behaves, and a closed cabin makes infections easy to pass on.

## What Hygieia can observe

An infection early warning: body temperature and resting heart rate rising together while heart-rate variability falls, for two of three days.

## What Hygieia cannot observe

Immune markers in blood, or which infection it is.
