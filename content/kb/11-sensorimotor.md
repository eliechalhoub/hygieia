## What it is

Balance and coordination adapt to weightlessness and must re-adapt after each change of gravity, such as landing on Mars.

## What Hygieia can observe

A weekly balance self-check, and extra balance checks after a gravity transition in the Mars surface profile.

## What Hygieia cannot observe

Inner-ear function directly.
