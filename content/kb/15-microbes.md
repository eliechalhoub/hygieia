## What it is

Microbes and the people carrying them both change in a closed spacecraft, which may affect infection risk.

## What Hygieia can observe

Nothing directly. The infection early warning is the closest related signal.

## What Hygieia cannot observe

The microbiome, or microbes on cabin surfaces.
