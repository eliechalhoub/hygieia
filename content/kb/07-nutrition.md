## What it is

The crew must eat and drink enough from a fixed food system for the whole mission.

## What Hygieia can observe

Logged energy intake, fluids and glucose. Two low-fluid days raise the dehydration alert.

## What Hygieia cannot observe

Nutrient status (vitamins, minerals) or body composition.
