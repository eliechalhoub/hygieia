## What it is

Isolation, confinement and distance from home can affect mood and behaviour over a long mission.

## What Hygieia can observe

A daily mood check-in and shared crew time, alongside sleep. Mood is a sharing category the astronaut can keep private.

## What Hygieia cannot observe

Any clinical condition. Hygieia never diagnoses; it shows drift from your own baseline and suggests small changes.

## Notes

The private journal is never shared and is not read by the flight surgeon or the AI assistant.
