## What it is

Shifted schedules, workload, noise and unusual light make sleep on missions short and irregular, which affects performance.

## What Hygieia can observe

Sleep hours, sleep regularity and daytime light from the actigraphy band (modelled on NASA's ARCHeR study on Artemis II). Two short nights can trigger a standing order from the flight surgeon.

## What Hygieia cannot observe

Sleep stages or sleep disorders.
