## What it is

Without gravity to work against, muscles and aerobic fitness decline unless the crew exercises. NASA reports that station crews exercise about two hours a day.

## What Hygieia can observe

Logged exercise minutes and daily activity from the actigraphy band, against the profile's exercise prescription.

## What Hygieia cannot observe

Actual strength or aerobic capacity. Hygieia sees adherence, not fitness test results.

## Notes

The Transit profile has no treadmill. NASA's Zero T2 study is examining how exercising without a treadmill affects health and performance; it is research in progress.
