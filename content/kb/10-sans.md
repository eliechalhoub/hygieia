## What it is

Changes to the eye and optic nerve seen in some astronauts on long flights. NASA describes it as its leading spaceflight-related health risk.

## What Hygieia can observe

A weekly vision self-check: gap-direction acuity trials, a grid, and two questions. Three falling results raise a prompt to repeat the check and tell the flight surgeon.

## What Hygieia cannot observe

The eye itself. Finding SANS needs imaging of the back of the eye. The self-check is a screening prompt, not a diagnosis.

## Notes

NASA's Thigh Cuff study is testing whether tight thigh cuffs keep more fluid in the legs, and its B Complex study is investigating a daily B vitamin supplement. Both are under investigation. Hygieia does not present either as a treatment.
