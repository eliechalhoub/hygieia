## What it is

Too little oxygen reaching the body, for example from a cabin atmosphere problem or a lower-pressure habitat.

## What Hygieia can observe

Blood oxygen (SpO₂) daily with a hard red limit, and cabin pressure and CO₂ on the crew dashboard. Low SpO₂ can trigger a standing order.

## What Hygieia cannot observe

The cause. A low reading can also be a cold finger or a loose sensor, so the first advice is to re-measure.

## Notes

The surface-habitat pressure and SpO₂ limits in the Lunar and Mars profiles are design assumptions.
